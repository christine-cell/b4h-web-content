This is your personal tracking tool for the whole program — and long after. Fill it in right here, week by week: tick your to-dos, write your goals, log your exercise and record your test results.

What you’ll do

  • Use one week of the workbook for each week of the program.
  • Record your baseline tests in Week 1 and re-test in Week 11 — the workbook shows your progress side by side.
  • Print it or save it as a PDF any time, filled in or blank.

Why use a workbook?

This workbook is an extra guide, designed to help you stay engaged and accountable. It isn’t required, but most participants find that writing things down makes the process much simpler and more concrete.

Track and measure

Use it to track and measure your results.

Stay accountable

It’s a valuable tool that encourages accountability, so you can reach the results you want.

Look back later

Refer back to it in the months or years to come. Redo the tests to see how you’ve progressed and what you may need to work on.

My goals

What do you want this program to help you do?

Small steps, real progress

Turn a goal into a small action you can do even on a hard day.

The 7 small-steps principles
  1. Start with your WHY — choose a goal that improves your daily life.
  2. Shrink the goal until it feels easy — five minutes counts.
  3. Focus on actions, not outcomes — consistency beats intensity.
  4. Attach the habit to your routine — after brushing your teeth, while the kettle boils.
  5. Track the win, not perfection — showing up counts.
  6. Celebrate small wins — you become someone who doesn’t quit.
  7. Miss a day? Reset. Don’t miss twice on purpose.

Mes objectifs

Qu’aimeriez-vous que ce programme vous aide à faire ?

Petits pas, vrais progrès

Transformez un objectif en une petite action réalisable même dans une journée difficile.

Les 7 principes des petits pas
  1. Commencez par votre POURQUOI — choisissez un objectif qui améliore votre quotidien.
  2. Réduisez l’objectif jusqu’à ce qu’il semble facile — cinq minutes, ça compte.
  3. Misez sur les actions, pas sur les résultats — la régularité l’emporte sur l’intensité.
  4. Rattachez l’habitude à votre routine — après le brossage de dents, pendant que l’eau bout.
  5. Notez la réussite, pas la perfection — être présent(e), ça compte.
  6. Célébrez les petites victoires — vous devenez quelqu’un qui n’abandonne pas.
  7. Vous avez manqué une journée ? Recommencez. Ne manquez pas deux fois exprès.
