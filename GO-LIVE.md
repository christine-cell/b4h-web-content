## ✅ Your morning checklist (~2 min)

### 1. ~~Enable GitHub Pages~~ — DONE ✅
I already enabled it: **Settings → Pages** shows *"building from the main branch"*, and it auto-detected the custom domain **training.boxing4health.com** from the `CNAME` file (status: *DNS Check in Progress*). The site is already live at the fallback URL: **https://christine-cell.github.io/b4h-web-content/**.
Once the DNS record below resolves, come back to Settings → Pages and turn on **Enforce HTTPS** (it's greyed out until DNS is verified).

### 2. Add the DNS record — the only thing standing between you and training.boxing4health.com
I did **not** touch your Wix domain settings (didn't want to fumble your live domain unattended). Add this one record in **Wix → your account → Domains → boxing4health.com → Manage/Edit DNS records**:
Add a **CNAME** record:
- **Host/Name:** `training`
- **Points to / Value:** `christine-cell.github.io`
- TTL: default
Leave all your existing `www` / root records untouched. Propagation can take 10 min–a few hours. Until then, the site is reachable at `https://christine-cell.github.io/b4h-web-content/`.

### 3. Review the French medical terms
Open `TRANSLATION-REVIEW.md`, confirm the flagged clinical terms, then clear the "translation under review" flag by setting `fr.reviewflag` to `""` in `assets/js/i18n.js` (both are already listed there).

## How to change things later
- Content/design rules live in `STYLE_GUIDE.md`; run edits through the `b4h-content` skill (`.claude/skills/b4h-content/`).
- Pages are generated by `tools/build_pages.py` (lessons) and `tools/build_hub.py` (hub) from `data/modules.json` + the French files in `i18n/`. Re-run them after edits, or just edit the built HTML directly for one-offs.
- Bump the `?v=` on CSS/JS links (or the `V` in the tools) when you change styles/scripts so browsers pick up changes.
