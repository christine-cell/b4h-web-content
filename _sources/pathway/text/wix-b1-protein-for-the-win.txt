Protein for
the win!
Here are some meal suggestions tailored to menopausal women
with Parkinson’s, focusing on boosting protein and fiber intake
while accommodating the timing for animal protein:
Breakfast Ideas
1- Greek Yogurt & Berry Parfait
Ingredients: Plain Greek yogurt, mixed berries (blueberries,
raspberries), chia seeds, and ground flaxseeds.
Notes: Greek yogurt offers protein; berries, chia, and
flaxseeds add fiber and omega-3s.
2- Avocado Toast with Hemp Hearts
Ingredients: Whole-grain toast, smashed avocado, hemp
hearts, and a sprinkle of nutritional yeast.
Notes: A high-fiber, plant-based start to the day.
3- Overnight Oats with Almond Butter
Ingredients: Rolled oats, unsweetened almond milk, almond
butter, and a topping of sliced banana or chopped walnuts.
Notes: High fiber from oats and protein from almond butter.
Veggie Scramble with Tofu or 3 Eggs
Ingredients: Firm tofu, spinach, tomatoes, bell peppers, and
turmeric.
Notes: A plant-based protein option with fiber-packed
veggies.
Smoothie
Ingredients: Plant-based protein powder, frozen spinach, ½ C
frozen berries, unsweetened almond milk, 1 tbsp chia seeds
or flax seeds and 1 tbsp nut butter of choice, almond milk/oat
milk
Lunch
1- Lentil and Quinoa Salad
Ingredients: Cooked lentils, quinoa, arugula, cherry
tomatoes, cucumbers, olive oil, and lemon juice.
Notes: Lentils and quinoa provide plant-based protein and
fiber.
2- Chickpea and Kale Wrap or Animal protein of choice in
wrap
Ingredients: Whole-grain tortilla, mashed chickpeas, kale,
shredded carrots, tahini, and lemon dressing.
Notes: Packed with fiber and plant protein.
3- Stuffed Bell Peppers
Ingredients: Bell peppers stuffed with black beans(or
ground beef), cooked quinoa, diced tomatoes, and
nutritional yeast.
Notes: Black beans and quinoa make it a complete protein
source.
4- Hearty Veggie and Lentil Soup or Thai chicken soup
Ingredients: Lentils(or chicken), celery, carrots, tomatoes,
spinach, and vegetable broth. Add curry paste, soya
sauce, coconut milk with chicken broth for thai version.
Notes: Perfect for fiber and plant protein.
Dinner Ideas
1- Animal protein- 25-30g, avocado, veggies (chicken, steak),
sweet potatoes and salad
2- Protein Shake 30-40g (vegan protein powder, almond
milk, water, 1/c berries or a banana, 1 tsp of nut butter
3- Grilled Salmon with Roasted Vegetables
Ingredients: Salmon roasted sweet potatoes, and
asparagus.
Notes: Salmon is high in omega-3s and protein; sweetpotatoes
and asparagus add fiber.
4- Zucchini Noodles with Turkey Meatballs
Ingredients: Spiralized zucchini, turkey meatballs,
marinara sauce, and a side salad with olive oil dressing.
Notes: Light on carbs but high in protein and fiber.
