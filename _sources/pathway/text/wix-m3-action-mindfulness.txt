Steps Toward Mindfulness
1. Start with 5 minutes a day and work your way up from there at a pace that feels
comfortable to you. Schedule time to do Mindfulness. I prefer to do it in the morning
when I get up, but for others, it may work better later in the day. Remember, if you don’t
schedule time for it won’t happen.
2. Now that you have a good understanding of Parkinson's Disease, the symptoms and the
importance of exercise, I want you to work on making a daily schedule for the next 30-60
days that includes exercise, mindfulness and sleep.
3. Ensure that you continue completing daily journal entries and that you are answering the
specific questions on a daily basis. It’s important that you start feeling more positive about
your situation and develop more confidence in being able to face the challenges, now,
and in the future.
