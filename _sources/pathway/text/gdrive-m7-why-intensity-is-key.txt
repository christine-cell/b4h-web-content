🧠 Reverses Brain Damage
Yale University research (2024) showed high-intensity exercise
can reverse neurodegeneration and increase dopamine-
producing neurons—not just slow decline, but actually restore
brain health.
💪 Intensity Matters Most
It's not just about moving—it's about how hard you work. High-
intensity exercise (80-85% max heart rate) triggers
neuroprotection that moderate exercise doesn't achieve.
Grows Brain Cells
🌱
High-intensity exercise increases BDNF (Brain-Derived
Neurotrophic Factor)—like fertilizer for your brain cells—
promoting survival, growth, and new neural connections.
🔬 No Drug Can Do This
Current medications only treat symptoms. High-intensity exercise
is the only intervention proven to potentially slow, stop, or
reverse disease progression at the cellular level.
⚠️ Inactivity Makes It Worse
Animal studies show that immobilization worsens Parkinson's
deficits. Not exercising at high intensity means missing the
chance to fight back neurologically.
The "Talk Test"
🎯
You should be able to speak in short sentences but not sing a
song. That's your target intensity—challenging but sustainable for
short periods.
"The medications we have available are only for symptomatic
treatment. They do not change the disease course. But exercise
seems to go one step beyond and protect the brain at the
neuronal level."
— Dr. Sule Tinaz, Associate Professor of Neurology, Yale School of
