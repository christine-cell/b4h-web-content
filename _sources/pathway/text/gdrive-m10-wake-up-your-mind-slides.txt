LESSON 10 · WAKE UP YOUR MIND
Cognitive
Impairment
in Parkinson's
Memory Thinking & Focus
What is cognitive impairment? A broad term referring
to difficulties with memory, thinking, concentration,
and other brain functions.
Mental Clarity
These challenges are greater than normal aging and can be
difficult to manage with current medical therapies alone.
Common in Parkinson's disease — and can significantly affect quality of life.
But here's the good news →
Exercise is one of the most
powerful tools
we have to support
brain function.
Research shows that problem solving, memory, and focus can improve immediately following exercise.

How Exercise Boosts
Your Brain
Regular exercise releases powerful brain chemicals and stimulates key memory structures:
BDNF
Hippocampus
Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor
Key area for learning & memory
Supports memory, learning, concentration & overall brain
health. Released during regular physical activity.
Responds strongly to aerobic exercise. Can actually grow
stronger as you become more physically fit.

Body + Mind:
Our Approach in Class
We intentionally incorporate exercises that challenge both the body and the mind at the same time.
The Dot-to-Dot Challenge
Flashcard Math While Moving
Fighters completed a numbered puzzle before class —
then the exact same puzzle immediately after. Every single
person beat their original time.
Volunteers walk around with flashcards containing math
problems. Fighters solve them while exercising — a whole
new level of multitasking.
