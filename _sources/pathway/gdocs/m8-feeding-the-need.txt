🌱 Easy Ways to Increase Fibre for Better Gut Health (and Sleep!) 💤
Inspired by Dr. Will Bulsiewicz, MD (gut health expert)
Most people massively under-eat fibre, and it shows up as bloating, constipation, inflammation, poor blood sugar control, weaker immunity—and yes, crappy sleep. Dr. B's message is simple: feed your gut bugs consistently and gently with plants.
🔬 Why Fibre Matters
Fibre is not about 'being regular.' It is about:
Feeding beneficial gut bacteria
Reducing inflammation
Improving insulin sensitivity
Supporting immune function
Regulating appetite and weight
Producing protective fatty acids
🎯 Goal: 30–40 g/day
📋 Dr. B's No-Drama Rules
1. Start Low, Go SlowIncrease gradually over 2–4 weeks.
2. Diversity > PerfectionAim for 20–30 different plant foods per week: fruits, veggies, beans, whole grains, nuts, seeds, herbs & spices.
3. Whole Foods FirstSupplements are optional. Real plants are mandatory.
🍽️ Easy, Low-Effort Ways to Add Fibre Daily
☀️ Breakfast
Add chia or flax to oatmeal
Add berries to smoothies
Choose oats over cereal
🌞 Lunch
Add ½ cup lentils to salads
Use whole-grain wraps
Add chickpeas to soups
🌙 Dinner & Snacks
Add one extra vegetable
Swap white rice for quinoa
Snack: fruit + nuts

Kiwi Nutrition (1–2 kiwis):
🌿 Fibre: 2–4 g (soluble + insoluble)
🛡️ Vitamin C for immunity & health
⚡ Actinidin enzyme aids digestion
✨ Improves stool consistency
💤 Sleep Benefits 💤
🌙 Natural melatonin
⏱️ Fall asleep faster
⭐ Improve sleep duration
✨ Improve sleep quality
🔑 Best Way to Use:
Eat 1–2 whole kiwis 30–60 minutes before bed
