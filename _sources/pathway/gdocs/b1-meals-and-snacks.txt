🥦  PROTEIN FOR THE WIN!
PPE Program  ·  Boxing 4 Health
Meal suggestions tailored to menopausal women with Parkinson's — focusing on boosting protein and fibre intake while accommodating medication timing.
🌅  BREAKFAST IDEAS

01  Greek Yogurt & Berry Parfait
Ingredients:
Plain Greek yogurt, mixed berries (blueberries, raspberries), chia seeds, ground flaxseeds
Why it works:
Greek yogurt delivers high-quality protein; berries, chia, and flaxseeds add fibre and omega-3s. A powerful anti-inflammatory start.

02  Avocado Toast with Hemp Hearts
Ingredients:
Whole-grain toast, smashed avocado, hemp hearts, nutritional yeast
Why it works:
A high-fibre, plant-based start. Hemp hearts add complete protein and hemp seed oil provides omega-3 fatty acids.

03  Overnight Oats with Almond Butter
Ingredients:
Rolled oats, unsweetened almond milk, almond butter, sliced banana or chopped walnuts
Why it works:
High fibre from oats, steady protein from almond butter. Prepare the night before for an effortless morning.

04  Veggie Scramble with Tofu or Eggs
Ingredients:
Firm tofu or 3 eggs, spinach, tomatoes, bell peppers, turmeric
Why it works:
Flexible plant-based or animal protein option. Turmeric adds anti-inflammatory benefits. Fibre-packed vegetables support gut health.

05  Power Smoothie
Ingredients:
Plant-based protein powder, frozen spinach, ½ cup frozen berries, almond or oat milk, 1 tbsp chia or flax seeds, 1 tbsp nut butter
Why it works:
Quick, high-protein, and easy to consume on difficult mornings. Adjust texture with extra liquid as needed.

🥗  LUNCH IDEAS

01  Lentil and Quinoa Salad
Ingredients:
Cooked lentils, quinoa, arugula, cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, extra-virgin olive oil, lemon juice
Why it works:
Lentils and quinoa together form a complete protein source with excellent fibre content. Olive oil is anti-inflammatory.

02  Chickpea & Kale Wrap
Ingredients:
Whole-grain tortilla, mashed chickpeas or protein of choice, kale, shredded carrots, tahini, lemon dressing
Why it works:
Packed with fibre and plant protein. Tahini adds calcium and healthy fats. Swap chickpeas for chicken or tuna if preferred.

03  Stuffed Bell Peppers
Ingredients:
Bell peppers stuffed with black beans or ground beef, cooked quinoa, diced tomatoes, nutritional yeast
Why it works:
Black beans and quinoa make a complete protein source. Nutritional yeast adds B vitamins including B12.

04  Hearty Lentil Soup or Thai Chicken Soup
Ingredients:
Lentils or chicken, celery, carrots, tomatoes, spinach, vegetable or chicken broth. For Thai: add curry paste, soy sauce, coconut milk
Why it works:
Warming, filling, and flexible. Both versions are rich in protein and fibre. The Thai version adds anti-inflammatory turmeric and ginger.

🍽️  DINNER IDEAS

01  Animal Protein Plate
Ingredients:
25–30g animal protein (chicken, steak, fish), avocado, roasted vegetables, sweet potato, side salad
Why it works:
Timed for the evening to avoid interference with levodopa absorption. A well-balanced plate with complete protein, complex carbs, and healthy fats.

02  Grilled Salmon with Roasted Vegetables
Ingredients:
Salmon fillet, roasted sweet potatoes, asparagus, olive oil, lemon
Why it works:
Salmon is one of the richest sources of omega-3 fatty acids and provides 30g+ protein per serving. Sweet potatoes and asparagus add fibre and potassium.

03  Zucchini Noodles with Turkey Meatballs
Ingredients:
Spiralized zucchini, turkey meatballs, marinara sauce, side salad with olive oil dressing
Why it works:
Lower in carbohydrates but high in protein and fibre. Turkey is a lean protein with high tryptophan content which supports sleep quality.

04  Evening Protein Shake
Ingredients:
30–40g vegan protein powder, almond milk, water, ½ cup berries or banana, 1 tsp nut butter
Why it works:
An easy option on low-appetite evenings. Ensures protein goals are met even when a full meal isn't possible.
