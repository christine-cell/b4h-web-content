Peanut Thai Gluten-Free Sauce
A rich, creamy, and flavorful sauce perfect for dipping, drizzling over stir-fries, or using as a marinade.
Ingredients:
½ cup natural peanut butter (unsweetened)
¼ cup coconut aminos
1 tbsp lime juice (or rice vinegar for a tangier kick)
1 tbsp maple syrup (or honey)
1-2 tsp sriracha (adjust for spice preference)
1 tsp grated ginger (or ½ tsp ground ginger)
1 clove garlic, minced
¼ cup warm water (adjust for desired consistency)
1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
1 tsp fish sauce (optional, for umami depth)
Instructions:
In a medium bowl, whisk together peanut butter and coconut aminos until smooth.
Add lime juice, maple syrup, sriracha, ginger, and garlic. Whisk well.
Gradually stir in warm water until you reach your desired consistency.
Finish with toasted sesame oil and fish sauce (if using).
Taste and adjust seasoning if needed—add more lime juice for acidity, maple syrup for sweetness, or sriracha for heat.
Ways to Use It:
As a dipping sauce for spring rolls or grilled chicken
Drizzled over stir-fried vegetables or noodles
As a marinade for tofu, shrimp, or beef
Tossed with shredded cabbage for a quick Thai-inspired slaw
**Keeps in the fridge for 2 weeks in sealed jar
