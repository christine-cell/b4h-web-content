Freezing of Gait (Video)

5 min read Updated 2026

Please watch these videos and keep an eye on the clients feet. Shuffling or freezing of gait an happen when walking is initiated or mid walk when the client stops. Freezing can also happen when there is a change in floor surface or a going through a doorway.


This is one client's first class with us and after one session he had improved his freezing with proper cueing. Having the client FEEL the changes you are showing them is key to them implementing these changes at home and not just in the gym.
