Class Breakdown Overview

2 min read Updated 2026

Our classes are broken down into the following- here


Warm up- 7-10min

High Intensity- Mix between functional exercises and Boxing technique and drills. 32-35min

Cool down- Gentle stretching and or ball work(using self massage balls) 10-15min


We have 3 types of classifications for our fighters.

Fighter

Champion

Champion Plus


**Once your assessment with the client is complete you will then be able to place the student in the appropriate class.


Our Fighter Classes- are higher intensity and are set up as circuit based classes.


-These classes are best for those that have low to moderate balance concerns. -Exercises are Parkinson’s specific and include amplitude-based movements and dual tasking. -Cognitive exercises will also be added in these classes.


Our Champion Classes- are lower intensity and are set up as a group class vs a circuit class. They are a mix of Champions and Champion Plus clients.
