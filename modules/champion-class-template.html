01 — Warm Up · 7–10 Minutes

🏋️
Space Note: If stations have been set up, use the whole gym as much as possible for warm-up and other exercises — get clients moving through the space rather than staying in one spot.
1
GO BIG — Moving Around the Gym
Same exercises as Fighter — but a chair must be accessible for every student. Encourage standing as much as possible, but all exercises can be done seated if needed.
  • Big Walk — around the square or gym perimeter
  • High Knees — exaggerated lift, arms swinging
  • Butt Kicks — upright posture, deliberate movement
  • Monster Walk — BIG lateral steps with full arm extension
🪑 Chair available at all times
2
GO BIG — Standing in Spot
Same exercises in place. Have clients place hands on the wall for balance assistance as needed. Use a chair when clients experience any of the following:
  • Fatigue
  • Dizziness
  • An OFF period
  • Back pain
  • Lack of strength to stand for an extended period
🪑 Wall or chair support encouraged
3
PWR Warm Up — Seated
A seated warm-up option using PWR! principles for clients who need a fully seated class. Coming Soon — check the Instructor Forum for updates when this resource is added.
⏳ Coming Soon
🥊 You have creative freedom here. Add your own warm-up elements as long as BIG movements and voice are always included — those two are non-negotiable in every Champion warm-up.

🎙️ Voice Exercises — Add to Every Warm Up

  • Vowel Sounds: A, E, I, O, U — full face involvement, hold as long as possible
  • Alphabet Letters in the Air: Draw each letter of the alphabet as BIG as they can while saying it out loud — great seated exercise for amplitude AND voice

02 — High Intensity · 32–35 Minutes

The Champion format uses the same exercises as Fighter — with purposeful modifications at every station. Notice how small adjustments make each exercise accessible without sacrificing therapeutic value.

Fighter Version

  • PWR ups from standing
  • Double 007 — full standing rotation
  • Lateral leg raises — standing
  • Full burpees
  • Core — floor or standing
  • Scarf toss in lunge — standing

Champion Modification

  • PWR ups from seated position
  • Double 007 — seated or supported rotation
  • Lateral leg raises using a chair
  • Modified burpees with chair → PWR up finish
  • Core — seated, breath & posture focus
  • Scarf toss in lunge — chair supported

Sample Circuit · 6 Stations

6 rounds · 2 minutes each · 30 sec–1 min rest between rounds

1
PWR! Ups from Seated
Full amplitude on the rise — chest open, arms extended, tall finish. The movement starts from the seat, not a standing position.
🪑 Champion Mod: Start seated. Focus on powerful push through the legs and full extension at the top.
2 MIN
2
Double 007 — Trunk Rotation
Add cognitive dual-task: count backwards from 100 in 3s, or call out vegetables starting with A through Z.
🪑 Champion Mod: Seated or with chair support. Eyes track the hands. Don't sacrifice the rotation for speed.
2 MIN
3
Lateral Leg Raises
Hip abduction with upright trunk — don't let the body lean into the raise.
🪑 Champion Mod: Use a chair for support. Focus on controlled lift and return — both directions are therapeutic.
2 MIN
4
Modified Burpees + PWR! Up Finish
The chair replaces the floor component — clients push up from a chair, step back (or march in place), then finish with a full PWR! up.
🪑 Champion Mod: Chair throughout. The finish — standing tall with full arm extension — is non-negotiable. That's the therapeutic payoff.
2 MIN
5
Core — Seated, Breath & Posture
Seated core work focused on breath coordination and postural awareness. Think tall spine, diaphragmatic breathing, gentle trunk activation.
🪑 Champion Mod: Fully seated. Focus on quality of breath and posture rather than intensity. This is a recovery station within the circuit.
2 MIN
6
Scarf Toss in Lunge — Switch Legs
Lunge position — scarf toss and catch. Switch legs. Balance, coordination, and reaction speed all in one.
🪑 Champion Mod: Chair nearby or hands on wall for balance during the lunge. Reduce lunge depth as needed — keep the scarf toss BIG.
2 MIN
👥
Struggling Student? If a student can't keep up or is struggling with instructions, have another instructor or volunteer work with them one-on-one. Break down movements — focus on legs first, then reintroduce arms. Never leave a struggling client to figure it out alone.

Boxing Combinations

Champion boxing uses a slightly different combination structure — varied rhythm, different patterns, and built-in recovery intervals. Adapt timing and rounds based on your group.

1
Jab · Cross · Jab · Cross + Switch Stance
Double the basics — then a stance switch. Gets both sides working from the start.
2
Jab · Cross · Big Backstep · Knee
The backstep adds footwork and spatial awareness before the knee — a big balance and coordination challenge.
3
Jab · Cross · Cross · Jab
Reversed rhythm — ends with the jab instead of the cross. Cognitive challenge in the familiar combination pattern.
4
Body · Body · Head · Head
Level changes — punch low then high. Requires trunk flexion and extension alternating with each combination.
5
10 Seconds On · 10 Seconds Off · × 3
Interval burst — maximum effort for 10 seconds, full rest for 10 seconds, repeat 3 times. Short enough to push hard, long enough to recover. Great for clients managing fatigue.
6
Big Boxing Freestyle
Open round — clients choose their own combinations. Freedom within the framework. Their choice, their confidence, their ownership of the movement.
🥊 Instructor's Discretion: Feel free to change and advance combinations with groups that are more comfortable. The combinations above are starting points — read your room and challenge clients appropriately.

03 — Cool Down · 5–10 Minutes

Focus on 3–5 stretches based on what you worked in class. For Champion clients, the cool-down is especially important — many experience blood pressure fluctuations and need a gradual transition out of intensity.

🧘
Yoga Techniques
Seated or standing yoga poses — especially chest openers and hip flexor stretches. Modify all poses for chair support.
🫁
Breathing Exercises
Extended exhale breathing, box breathing, or diaphragmatic work. Especially beneficial for voice, respiratory function, and nervous system regulation.
⚽
Ball Releases
Therapy ball / massage ball on back, chest, feet, or shoulders. Seated ball work is fully effective — no need to stand.
🤸
Targeted Stretches
Based on what you used in class — hip flexors after sit-stands, chest after boxing, hamstrings after leg raises.
📹
Champion Class Video: A Champion class ideas video is available to watch — use it to observe pacing, chair usage, modification style, and how the instructor maintains energy and connection with the group throughout.
