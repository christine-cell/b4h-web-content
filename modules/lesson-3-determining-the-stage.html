How do I determine what stage a student is at? Here


Screen a student over the phone or in person with the following questions so you can properly assess which class, High or Low functioning, they would be best suited as their Trial Class. If you unsure, use your discretion to book an assessment beforehand OR have them come in to watch a class to observe how they move and get a better idea of their physical limitations. See prospect screening questions attached.


Prospective client questionaire.docx.pdfDownload PDF • 86KB

Initial Assessment- After a trial class, this should be completed before the student can start classes. Every student you see will have different health concerns and abilities so it’s important that a full health assessment be done after they have had their trial class.


Parkinson’s Assessment- Seniors should have the same assessment if they are concerned about their balance, being a fall risk or wanting to have baseline measurements to track their progress.
