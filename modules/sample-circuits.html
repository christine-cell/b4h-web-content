Sample CircuitsExemples de circuits

10 min read Updated 2026

These are ready-to-use circuits to get you started — drop them straight into class or adapt them freely. Each one is the high-intensity portion only: four Fighter sample circuits with paired Functional and Boxing stations, plus two complete class plans contributed by B4H instructors so you can see how different coaches bring their own style to the same framework.

What you'll learn

  • Run four Fighter sample circuits, station by station, with the right rounds and timing for each.
  • Set up paired Functional and Boxing stations and know what equipment each one calls for.
  • Layer in cognitive, dual-task, and partner challenges that make each station richer.
  • Deliver two full class plans — warm-up, functional, boxing, and cool-down — from real instructors.
  • Use student-led and freestyle moments to build ownership, engagement, and fun.

Safety

These are circuits only — they do not include warm-up or cool-down. Always bookend them with your VIGOR-based warm-up and a proper cool-down and release session.

Functional Stations

  1. High to Low Rows with Bands

    Resistance band anchored high — pull down and back. Full shoulder and back engagement.

    Resistance Band

  2. Big Walk/Run + Bridge to Plank

    Across the front of the gym, then down to bridge position and hold plank.

  3. Crawling + PWR! Steps

    Bear crawl or modified crawl into powerful PWR! up steps.

  4. Plank Hold + Mountain Climbers

    Hold plank for 10 sec (count out loud, slow) then 10 mountain climbers — or roll over for modification.

  5. Power Squat with Medicine Ball

    Squat with med ball — pass ball to partner while opening chest on the way up. Partner option: open chest while other squats.

    Medicine Ball

Boxing Stations

  1. Freestyle on the Bobs

    Open freestyle on bob bags — encourage full amplitude and footwork.

    Bob Bags

  2. Cognitive + Jab Cross on Bags

    Call out things on signs while punching: "4 movie titles" / "3 things in your fridge" — dual-task challenge.

  3. Ground & Pound + Speed Bag Switch

    One partner: ground & pound bag. Other partner: 10 punches on speed bag. Switch.

    Speed Bag Floor Bag

  4. Bosu Boxing + Open Chest

    Squat on Bosu ball, punch while balanced. Open chest between combinations.

    Bosu Ball

  5. Squat Front Kicks + Hooks

    10 squat front kicks, then 10 hooks left/right.

Functional Stations

  1. Cable Back Rows — Standing

    Standing row with full scapular retraction. Upright posture throughout.

    Cable Machine

  2. Burpees Over the Bag

    Burpee, step over bag, burpee on the other side. Repeat. Amplitude in the step-over.

  3. Single Arm Shoulder Press on Ball

    Sit on stability ball — single arm overhead press. Balance challenge plus shoulder strength.

    Stability Ball Dumbbell

  4. Squat with PVC Pipe / Broomstick

    Rest PVC pipe or broomstick on shoulders for posture alignment during squat. A dollar-store broomstick works perfectly.

    PVC Pipe or Broomstick

  5. Airplane Balance + Arm Swing

    Single leg balance in airplane (hinge) position with counter-rotating arm swing.

Boxing Stations

  1. Freestyle — Double End Bags

    Open freestyle on double end bags — speed, reaction, and coordination focus.

    Double End Bags

  2. Twist & Punch + Stance Switch

    10 twist punches, then switch sides with a big knee turn or jump switch.

  3. PWR! Centre Stance → Reverse Lunge → Front Kick

    Full combination: PWR! up from centre, reverse lunge, front kick. Power plus balance plus amplitude.

  4. Memory Game + Bag Punching

    Call out things on your own bucket list while punching the bag. Personal dual-task — great for engagement.

  5. Bosu Ball Balance Punches — On Knees

    Kneel on Bosu ball — punch from kneeling position. Core stability plus upper body power.

Twice through each side — 1.5 minutes each.

Functional Stations

  1. Crawling + Power Steps

    Bear crawl or modified crawl leading into powerful PWR! steps.

  2. Surrender

    From standing, kneel one knee, kneel both, stand one, stand tall. Powerful lower body plus core challenge.

  3. Stock the Shelves

    Reaching movements at various heights — simulates placing objects on shelves. Functional amplitude training.

  4. Smovey Rings — Double & Alternating

    20 reps double swing → 20 reps alternating swing → 10 Smovey squats.

    Smovey Rings

Boxing Stations

  1. Punch Ladder — Count Out Loud

    1 punch, 2 punches, up to 10, then back down to 1. Count every rep out loud — voice plus boxing combined.

  2. Toe Touch → Back Step → Knee → Switch

    Toe touch, big back step, knee strike, switch stance. Full body flow with balance challenge.

  3. Freestyle

    Open freestyle round — clients choose their combinations. Ownership and confidence.

  4. Jab, Knee, Hook, Cross

    Full 4-hit combination — requires coordination, stance work, and trunk rotation on the hook.

Coach's Corner

Student-Led Moment: Have students come up with the functional exercises themselves — 10 of each, then 5 of each. This builds ownership, cognitive engagement, and a sense of fun. Great for groups that know each other well.

Functional Stations

  1. Jumping Jacks

    10 reps → 5 reps — or marching jacks for a modified version.

  2. Push Ups

    10 reps → 5 reps — wall, incline, or floor based on client ability.

  3. Superman

    10 reps → 5 reps — prone back extension, arms forward. Postural extensor strength.

  4. Power Squats

    10 reps → 5 reps — full amplitude, arms out, BIG finish on the rise.

Boxing Stations

  1. Freestyle

    Open round — full amplitude, own combinations.

  2. Squat + Front Kick

    Power squat, explosive front kick. Lower body power sequence.

  3. Bosu Balance Boxing

    Balance on Bosu, punch from stable position. Core plus upper body combined.

    Bosu Ball

  4. Ground & Pound + Partner Speed Bag

    Hit bag on the ground, partner does 10 speed bag hits, switch. Then 2 reverse lunges OR 1 surrender.

    Floor Bag Speed Bag

  5. Call Out Things While Punching

    Instructor calls a category — clients shout answers while punching. Cognitive plus voice plus boxing all at once.

Full Class Example · Champion

Full Champion Class — Seated & Standing Format

Warm Up

  1. Breathing

    Deep inhale-exhale × 5.

  2. Tongue Twister + Palm Rub

    Rub palms together while repeating "Four fine fresh fish for you" — warms voice and hands simultaneously.

  3. Shoulder Rolls

    Forward and back — loosen the upper body before movement begins.

Functional — Seated & Standing

  1. Seated Leg Extensions

    Nice and tall, arms on hips. Extend one leg forward, tap toes to floor, return. 10 reps each side → 10 alternating → add heel tap on return. 10 alternating.

  2. Apple Picking

    Legs spread, right arm stretches up and across — reach for an imaginary apple and pull it down to the opposite side. 10 reps each side. Can also be done standing.

  3. Sit to Stand × 3

    Then walk around the chair with big steps and stand at the back of the chair.

  4. Big Back Steps

    Holding chair back for balance — stretch leg as far back as possible. 10 each side → 10 alternating.

  5. Side Open Back Steps

    Big open step reaching toward the back wall (like opening a door). Arm and leg go around and back, eyes follow the hand. Return. 10 each side.

Boxing

30 sec freestyle warm-up.

  1. Combo 1

    Right foot front → Jabs ×10 → Cross ×10 → switch foot. 3 sets. PWR UP!

  2. Combo 2

    Right foot → Jab ×2, Cross ×1 → switch side. 8 sets each.

  3. Combo 3

    Right foot → Cross ×2, Jab ×1 → switch side. 8 sets each.

  4. Combo 4 — Knees on Bag

    Right leg ×10 → left leg ×10 → alternating ×10.

  5. Combo 5

    Double straight punches ×10 each side.

  6. Finish

    30 sec freestyle → PWR UP! Variation: Freeze Dance — music on = freestyle punching, music off = PWR UP! pose. Great for reaction and fun.

Cool Down — Seated

  1. Big Breathings

    Return to seated position.

  2. Hands Explosion Exercises

    Finger extension and spread.

  3. Wrist Rolls

    Both directions.

  4. Leg Extensions / Gas Pedal Exercises

    Seated lower-limb activation.

  5. Spine Stretching

    Flexion and extension exercises.

Full Class Example · Fighter

Full Fighter Circuit Class — Equipment-Based Stations

Functional Stations

  1. Balanced Squats on Bosu

    Step up (legs hip-width), find balance and tall posture, arms out front at shoulder height. Squat ×10. Rest 5 sec.

    Bosu Ball

  2. Box Step — PD Warrior Exercise

    Start on back dots, step in box formation (Forward R → Forward L → Back R → Back L). BIG open hands, wrists extended. Arms reach out with right leg step, pull in to chest on left. 5 reps, 5 sec active pause.

    Poly Spot Markers

  3. Balance XL — Superman Pose with Smovey Rings

    Stand tall, swing rings parallel while lifting one leg back straight. Hinge forward simultaneously. Core and glutes tight, head is extension of spine. Move leg up and down while swinging. 10 reps → PWR UP!

    Smovey Rings

  4. 007 with Pool Noodles

    Feet wide, hold pool noodle in trigger grip, arms outstretched at shoulder height. Rotate both hands to one side, pivot on one foot, return and repeat. 5 reps each side.

    Pool Noodle

  5. Power Steps with Punches

    Big step forward, arms out with big finger stretches, hold and punch ×6 alternating. Push back to centre, repeat other leg. Add tongue twister for dual-task variation.

    Floor Markers

  6. Banded Side Steps

    Legs hip-width, big step to side, simultaneously outstretch arms into T-pose pulling band — open chest fully. Hold briefly, bring arms and feet back in. 5 reps.

    Resistance Band

Boxing Stations

  1. Alternating Stance

    Jabs ×10 → Hooks ×10 → PWR UP!

  2. Uppercut & Hook Series

    Uppercut ×8 → Hook ×8 → Toe Touch ×10 → PWR UP! Switch stance.

  3. Jab, Cross, Hook, Up Combo

    Jab ×2, Cross ×2, Hook ×1, Up ×1 → PWR UP! Switch stance.

  4. Ladder Punches

    Count up 1–10, then back down 10–1. PWR UP!

  5. Superman Double Punch

    Alternating legs, superman ×10. PWR UP!

  6. Level Changes

    Alternating arms — Hooks to head ×8 → Jabs to chest ×8 → Hooks to ribs ×8 → alternating knees on bag ×8.

Key takeaways

  • These sample circuits are the high-intensity portion only — always bookend them with a VIGOR-based warm-up and a proper cool-down and release.
  • Each Fighter circuit pairs Functional and Boxing stations, with rounds and per-station timing set out for you to follow or adapt.
  • Equipment ranges from bands, Bosu balls, and Smovey rings to everyday items like a dollar-store broomstick or pool noodle — adapt to what your gym has.
  • Cognitive, dual-task, and voice challenges (call-outs, tongue twisters, counting out loud) turn every station into brain-and-body training.
  • Student-led and freestyle moments build ownership, engagement, and fun — and the full class examples show how each instructor makes the framework their own.
