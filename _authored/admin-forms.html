Section 01

Two Forms Before You Open

Every B4H program needs a Health History form and a Liability Waiver in place before your first class. These are essential for participant safety and for your own protection as an instructor.

The forms below are the ones we use at Boxing 4 Health HQ, provided as examples. You're welcome to use them as-is or adapt them to fit your specific location and setup. If you already have your own health history or liability forms — through your facility, your professional regulatory body, or your own practice — feel free to use those instead.

Good to know

The important thing is that some version of both a health history form and a liability waiver is in place before you open registration.
