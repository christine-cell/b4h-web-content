What’s inside the program

Everything is organized by topic, so it’s easy to find and ready when you are. There are 12 modules, plus bonus libraries with extra resources.

1 · Welcome & Getting Started

Start here — get oriented, take your baseline tests, and set your goals.

2 · Understanding Parkinson’s

Diagnosed with Parkinson’s — now what? Learn the basics, build your care team, and get to know dopamine.

3 · Stress, Mindset & Your Nervous System

The good, the bad and the ugly of stress — and simple ways to calm your nervous system.

4 · Sleep Support

Good sleep is a reachable dream — understand sleep with Parkinson’s and build a better routine.

5 · Balance & Confidence

Build balance, reduce your risk of falling, and move with confidence.

6 · Motivation & Mental Strength

Find your why, lean on your community, and keep moving forward.

7 · Exercise

Why exercise is non-negotiable, how hard to work, and your full movement library.

8 · Nutrition & Gut Health

Feed your brain, support your gut, and feel the difference.

9 · Voice, Facial Expression & Communication

If you don’t use it, you lose it — keep your voice strong and your expressions vibrant.

10 · Cognitive Fitness

Keep your mind sharp with cognitive challenges and dual-tasking.

11 · Supplements & Immune Support

What supplements may help, and how to build a strong immune system.

12 · You Did It!

Celebrate how far you’ve come and re-test to see your progress.
