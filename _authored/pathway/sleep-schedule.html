A simple sleep schedule helps you stay accountable — and shows you how long it takes to find a sleep routine that works for you.

What you’ll do

  • Download and print your sleep schedule.
  • Write in your wake-up time, your mindfulness or journal time, and your go-to-sleep time each day.
  • Watch how long it takes you to settle into a schedule that works for you.

What’s on the schedule

Each week has a column for every day, Monday to Sunday, with a spot to write the date. Every day you fill in three things:

RowWhat to write
Wake up TimeThe time you got up that morning.
Mindfulness/JournalYour wind-down, mindfulness or journal time that day.
Go to Sleep TimeThe time you went to sleep that night.

How to use it

  1. Print it

    Download one of the schedules below and keep it by your bed or on the fridge.

  2. Write in your times

    Start by writing in your actual wake-up and sleep times each day. Writing them down keeps you accountable.

  3. Add your wind-down

    Fill in the Mindfulness/Journal row when you take time to relax before bed — the Wind-Down Session is a great place to start.

  4. Switch to check marks

    Once your times stay consistent, check marks are great too.

Christine’s tip

Write in the times to stay accountable to this schedule and to see how long it takes you to get on a schedule that works for you. Start off by writing in your sleep times, but if it stays consistent then check marks are great too.
