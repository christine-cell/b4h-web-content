Protein & your meds

Protein and Parkinson’s meds don’t always play nicely together. I use vegan protein powder — no issues! If you use animal protein, take your pill 2 hours away from that meal.

Why protein matters

As we age, protein becomes essential — for muscle mass, bone health, immune function, hormone balance and fighting sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss). Don’t skip it!

Women — this one’s for you

Protein isn’t just for men. I actually think it’s even MORE important for women.

Ladies, listen up!

After 45, the hormonal shifts of menopause can speed up muscle loss and weaken bones. Protein fights back — supporting muscle, metabolism, weight management and bone density. It’s not optional, it’s essential. Make it a daily priority and your future self will thank you.

Quick reminder about your meds

Vegan protein powder (like the one I use from Costco) is totally fine with your Parkinson’s medication — no waiting needed! If you do eat animal protein (eggs, chicken, fish), try to take your levodopa at least 2 hours before or after that meal whenever possible.

High-protein MIND diet recipes

All brain-friendly, all delicious, all simple. Here we go!

Preteen-approved — my whole family of 4 loves these!

High protein · Dairy-free · Gluten-free option · About 25–30 g protein per serving

Ingredients

  • 2 scoops vegan protein powder (I use Costco’s)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup almond milk
  • 2 tbsp nut butter
  • 1½ cups rolled oats (I use Costco gluten-free oats — regular works too!)
  • 1 ripe banana
  • Optional: a handful of chocolate chips

Method

Blend everything together. Spray your waffle maker generously with coconut oil — don’t be shy or they’ll stick! Pour in the batter and cook 3–4 minutes until golden. That’s it. Seriously.

Blueberries are a MIND diet superstar — packed with brain-protective antioxidants.

High protein · MIND diet · Dairy-free · 5 minutes · About 22 g protein per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 scoop vegan protein powder
  • 1 cup frozen blueberries (or mixed berries)
  • 1 cup almond or oat milk
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • 1 tbsp ground flaxseed
  • ½ frozen banana
  • A handful of spinach (you won’t taste it!)

Method

Throw everything in the blender and blitz until smooth. Drink right away, or pour into a jar and refrigerate overnight for a grab-and-go breakfast.

Make a big pot on Sunday — eat all week. Legumes are a MIND diet must!

High protein · MIND diet · Vegan · About 18 g protein per bowl

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups red lentils, rinsed
  • 1 can diced tomatoes
  • 4 cups vegetable broth
  • 2 big handfuls of fresh spinach
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp turmeric
  • Olive oil, salt and pepper
  • A squeeze of lemon to finish

Method

Sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil for 3 minutes. Add the spices, then the lentils, tomatoes and broth. Simmer 20 minutes until the lentils are soft. Stir in the spinach and lemon juice. Done! Freezes beautifully.

Fatty fish is a top MIND diet food. Omega-3s for your brain, protein for your muscles.

High protein · MIND diet · 10 minutes · About 40 g protein per bowl

Ingredients

  • 1 salmon fillet (fresh or canned wild salmon works great!)
  • 1 cup cooked brown rice or quinoa
  • A big handful of leafy greens or kale
  • ½ avocado, sliced
  • A handful of cherry tomatoes
  • A drizzle of olive oil + a squeeze of lemon
  • Salt, pepper and everything bagel seasoning

Method

Pan-sear or bake the salmon 8–10 minutes. Build your bowl: grains first, then greens, salmon, avocado and tomatoes. Drizzle with olive oil and lemon. Done in the time it takes to cook the rice!

Walnuts are a MIND diet superstar — eat them daily if you can!

High protein · MIND diet · Vegan · No cooking · About 18 g protein per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • ¼ cup walnuts, roughly chopped
  • 2 cups mixed greens or arugula
  • ½ cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • ¼ red onion, thinly sliced
  • ¼ cup parsley or fresh herbs
  • 3 tbsp olive oil + 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard + salt and pepper

Method

Whisk together the olive oil, lemon and mustard for the dressing. Toss everything else together in a big bowl, drizzle over the dressing and toss again. Zero cooking required — done in 5 minutes flat.

Make it tonight, eat it tomorrow. Breakfast is done before you wake up!

High protein · MIND diet · Dairy-free · 5 minutes prep · About 25 g protein per jar

Ingredients

  • ½ cup rolled oats (gluten-free if needed)
  • 1 scoop vegan protein powder
  • ¾ cup almond or oat milk
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 1 tbsp almond butter
  • ½ cup blueberries or strawberries (fresh or frozen)
  • A drizzle of maple syrup or honey, to taste

Method

Stir the oats, protein powder, milk, chia seeds and almond butter together in a jar. Pop the lid on and refrigerate overnight. In the morning, top with berries and a little sweetener. Grab and go!
