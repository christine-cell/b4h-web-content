Section 01 · The basics

What are supplements?

Dietary supplements are substances used to add nutrients to your diet or to help with certain health concerns. These can include digestive problems, osteoporosis (thinning bones), arthritis, or general nutrient deficiencies.

If you’re looking to support your health, getting the right amount of vitamins and minerals each day is essential. Used appropriately, supplements may help support your energy, mood, mental clarity and overall well-being.

Supplements come in many forms:

Pills

Capsules

Powders

Gel tabs

Liquids

Good to know

Supplements should never replace a healthy diet. If you’re eating a whole-foods diet — focusing on nutrient-dense foods and limiting inflammatory foods and excess dairy — you’re already one big step ahead.

A healthy diet supports your gut, and a healthy gut plays an important role in supporting your immune system. Supplements work best as an addition to good nutrition, not a substitute for it.

Section 02 · Gut health

Gut health & probiotics

One of the first places I like to start when we talk about supplements is gut health — and that often brings us to probiotics.

Probiotics are beneficial bacteria that can help support a healthy balance in your digestive system. They may help if you have digestive concerns such as indigestion, bloating or constipation.

Your gut naturally contains a mix of good and bad bacteria. Probiotics help replenish the beneficial bacteria that keep your digestive system working properly, and a high-quality probiotic supplement can help restore that balance.

For example, if you have ever:

Taken antibiotics

These can wipe out beneficial bacteria along with the harmful ones.

Had a stomach virus

Illness can throw off your gut’s natural balance.

Had a parasite or serious digestive illness

These situations can disrupt the balance of bacteria in your gut.

Good to know

Many people don’t need to stay on probiotics forever. They’re often used for a period of time to help improve gut health, depending on your individual needs.

Section 03 · Food as medicine

Foods that support gut health

Along with supplements, certain foods can also help support the beneficial bacteria in your gut.

Bone broth

Ideally grass-fed and organic. Rich in collagen and gut-healing compounds.

Kimchi

Fermented vegetables packed with beneficial bacteria.

Sauerkraut

Fermented cabbage — a classic probiotic-rich food.

Kefir

A fermented milk drink — not ideal for everyone. Introduce it gradually.

A word of caution on fermented foods

Fermented foods aren’t right for everyone. If you have underlying digestive issues, they can sometimes make symptoms worse. That’s why it’s important to introduce these foods gradually, ideally with guidance from a health professional. Understanding the root cause of digestive symptoms is always the first step.

Section 04 · Immune support

Supporting your immune system

I also want you to think about something important.

Most of us prepare for winter by getting out warm clothes, changing tires and getting our homes ready for colder weather. But how often do we prepare our bodies for cold and flu season?

Winter prep — for your body

  • Warm clothes for the cold
  • Winter tires on the car
  • Preparing your home
  • Preparing your body — don’t forget this one!

Supporting your immune system year-round becomes especially important in the colder months. Certain supplements and food choices may help support your immune system and may reduce how long symptoms last, or how severe they are, when you do get sick.

Before starting any supplement, please check with your health professional to make sure there are no contraindications (reasons it could be unsafe) with your medications or health conditions.

Key takeaways

  • Supplements add to a healthy diet — they never replace it.
  • Probiotics help restore beneficial gut bacteria, for example after antibiotics or a stomach virus, and are often used for a period of time rather than forever.
  • Bone broth, kimchi, sauerkraut and kefir can support gut health — but introduce fermented foods gradually.
  • Prepare your body for cold and flu season, just like you prepare your home and car.
  • Always check with your care team before starting a new supplement.
