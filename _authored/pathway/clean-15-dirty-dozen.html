Not all produce carries the same amount of pesticide residue. These two short lists — updated for 2026 — help you see at a glance which fruits and vegetables tend to be the most and the least contaminated.

What you’ll do

  • Get to know the Dirty Dozen: the 12 fruits and vegetables with the most pesticide residue.
  • Get to know the Clean 15: the 15 with the least.
  • Download the list and keep it handy for your next grocery trip.

The two lists

Updated January 2026

The Dirty Dozen

The 12 most contaminated fruits and vegetables.

  1. Spinach
  2. Strawberries
  3. Kale, collards and mustard greens
  4. Grapes
  5. Peaches
  6. Cherries
  7. Nectarines
  8. Pears
  9. Apples
  10. Blackberries
  11. Blueberries
  12. Potatoes

The Clean 15

The 15 least contaminated (pesticides).

  1. Pineapples
  2. Sweet corn
  3. Avocados
  4. Papaya
  5. Onions
  6. Sweet peas (frozen)
  7. Asparagus
  8. Cabbage
  9. Watermelon
  10. Cauliflower
  11. Bananas
  12. Mangoes
  13. Carrots
  14. Mushrooms
  15. Kiwi
