Voice Lesson with Phil

Why start now

A softer or hoarse voice can be one of the first symptoms you experience with Parkinson’s. It is part of the widespread motor (movement) symptoms of Parkinson’s, such as tremor, bradykinesia (slowness of movement), rigidity (stiffness) and reduced amplitude (smaller range) of movement. It also affects the structures and movements of your facial expressions and your vocal cords.

So the sooner you get to work on keeping them active, the better your voice will be!
