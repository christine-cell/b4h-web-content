Ready to put the Mediterranean way of eating into action? Here are 10 days of simple, brain-friendly meals with recipes — plus a quick game to help you remember what to eat often and what to limit.

What you’ll do

  • Test yourself: sort everyday foods into “every day”, “a few times a week” and “limit or avoid”.
  • Follow a 10-day meal plan with a recipe for every breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack.
  • Build a grocery list for the days you’re shopping for — grouped by store section.

Christine’s tip

These are just ideas to get you started! Each recipe is made for one person — simply double it for two. Make a big batch of soups, stews and meatballs and freeze portions for busy days.

Talk to your care team

This plan is for education only and isn’t medical advice. Check with your doctor, neurologist, pharmacist or dietitian before making big changes to your diet — especially if you have diabetes, kidney problems, allergies or trouble swallowing, or if protein affects how your levodopa works.

Key takeaways

  • Fill most of your plate with plants: vegetables, fruit, legumes, whole grains, nuts and seeds — with olive oil as your main fat.
  • Have protein at every meal, and time animal protein 1–2 hours after your levodopa.
  • Fibre and plenty of water help keep your gut moving.
  • This program suggests going dairy-free — see Dairy & Parkinson’s for why.
  • Small steps count: try one new day at a time and tick it off.
