Recipe 01 · Gut health · Immunity

Instant Pot chicken bone broth

A mineral-rich tonic that boosts your immune system, improves digestion, and is rich in collagen and glucosamine — helping ease achy joints, rehydrate skin and build stronger bones. Homemade is always best.

~3 hrs
total time
1
whole chicken carcass
7 days
in the fridge — and it freezes beautifully

The bones

  • Bones, skin and cartilage from 1 roast chicken
  • Gizzards, neck, any extras
  • Chicken feet if available (from your butcher)

The extras

  • Onions
  • Carrots
  • Celery
  • Garlic
  • Herbs: rosemary, thyme, sage, bay leaves
  • High-quality sea salt and pepper
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar (helps break down the bones)
  • Filtered water, to just cover

Quick method

  1. Add the bones, veggies, herbs, salt and 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar to the Instant Pot. Cover with cold filtered water (1 inch over the bones — no more).
  2. Select the “Soup” setting and manually set it to 119 minutes (or High Pressure, 120 minutes).
  3. Let the pressure release naturally (about 20 minutes), or use quick release if you’re in a hurry.
  4. Strain through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a heat-safe glass pitcher. Push on the bones to extract all the broth.
  5. Skim off the fat cap (or refrigerate first and remove the solid fat cap). Store in mason jars — leave 1 inch of space if freezing!

Christine’s tip

I cook my broth for 2 hours minimum — 30 minutes makes chicken broth, not the rich, thick, might-even-gel-in-the-fridge bone broth you want. The more time, the better!

  • Extremely high in collagen, glycine and amino acids
  • Slow-simmered for maximum nutrient extraction
  • Shelf-stable, safe non-toxic packaging — great for travel
  • Actually tastes really good!

Recipe 02 · Anti-inflammatory · Brain health

Anti-inflammatory golden milk

Curcumin — turmeric’s most powerful compound — has medicinal properties used to reduce joint pain, arthritis and inflammation, and even to treat depression. This warm drink is your daily dose of golden goodness.

20 min
total time
1
serving
Dairy-free
made with coconut or almond milk

Golden milk may help to:

  • Reduce inflammation
  • Relieve joint pain
  • Improve digestion
  • Support liver health
  • Ease IBS (irritable bowel syndrome) symptoms
  • Lift your mood
  • Reduce bloating
  • Lower cholesterol

Ingredients

  • 1 cup organic full-fat coconut milk (BPA-free can) — or — 1 cup unsweetened almond milk + 1 tsp coconut oil
  • ½ tsp ground turmeric
  • Big pinch freshly ground black pepper (activates the curcumin!)
  • 1 small piece fresh ginger root (¼ inch, peeled and grated)
  • 1 big pinch ground cardamom
  • ¼ tsp raw honey — added off the heat (optional)

Instructions

  1. Blend or whisk all the ingredients together.
  2. Warm over medium heat for 15 minutes. Let it gently simmer — do not boil.
  3. For a frothier drink, blend in a high-speed blender before serving.
  4. Add the honey off the heat to preserve its enzymes.

Why black pepper?

The piperine in black pepper increases curcumin absorption by up to 2000%. Never skip it! Even a couple of grinds makes a huge difference.

Recipe 03 · Immunity · Energy · Anti-inflammatory

Immune-boosting wellness smoothie

Packed with fresh ginger, turmeric root, spinach, pineapple and banana, this smoothie is absolutely delicious, great for digestion, and a daily boost for your immune system. I drink this every day!

5 min
total time
2
smoothies
~139
calories each · plant-based

Ingredients

  • 1 banana
  • 1 cup frozen pineapple
  • 2–3 cups organic spinach
  • 1 knob fresh ginger, peeled (about 2 inches)
  • 1 knob fresh turmeric root (about 2 inches)
  • ½ tbsp manuka or raw honey
  • ⅛ tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • ½–¾ cup almond milk or coconut milk

Instructions

  1. Add all the ingredients to a large high-powered blender.
  2. Blend on high for 1–2 minutes until completely smooth.
  3. Add more almond milk if needed to reach the consistency you like.
  4. Pour and enjoy — makes 2 smoothies!

Swaps & add-ons

Add a scoop of collagen protein powder — it has no taste and adds a great protein boost! No bananas? Swap in a cored apple instead.

Key takeaways

  • The wellness smoothie is a quick, delicious daily immune boost.
