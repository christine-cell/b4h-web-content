You don’t have to navigate Parkinson’s alone. In this lesson, Christine talks about building your care team — and you’ll get a simple checklist to keep everyone’s contact information in one place.

Your Care Team: Who Do You Call?

Who is on your team?

These are the people the Support Team Checklist suggests you think about. You may already have some of them. Others you may need, or be working on finding.

Family doctor (GP)

Your general physician.

Neurologist

Ideally a Movement Disorder Specialist — a neurologist who focuses on conditions like Parkinson’s.

Naturopathic Doctor or Functional Medicine Practitioner

Someone you can ask for specific supplement recommendations, based on your health history, diet and lifestyle.

Nutritionist or Dietitian

Another good person to ask for specific recommendations about your diet and supplements.

Exercise professional

Look for a Parkinson’s-specialized exercise class.

Physiotherapist

A physiotherapist for your movement needs.

Massage Therapist

Look for experience in myofascial release.

Chiropractor (optional)

An optional addition to your team.

Osteopath

Look for experience in craniosacral work.

Peer support group

A support group of peers living with Parkinson’s.

Other

Anyone else who supports your health and well-being.

Christine’s tip

With the right tools and community behind you, there is so much strength to be found. Your care team is part of that community.

How to use the checklist

  1. Mark where you stand

    For each provider, tick Have, Need or Working on.

  2. Write down their details

    Record each provider’s name, phone number, email and address, plus any notes.

  3. Keep it handy

    Use it as your contact page, so all your important health care information is in one place.

Completing the Support Team Checklist is one of your Week 2 to-do items in your Action Plan workbook.

Key takeaways

  • You don’t have to navigate Parkinson’s alone — build a team around you.
  • Your team can include medical, nutrition, movement and hands-on providers, plus a peer support group.
  • Use the checklist to see who you have, who you need and who you’re working on.
  • Keep all your providers’ contact details in one place.
