GRAND Movements

Move big to move normally

“Grand Movements” means large-amplitude (big-range), whole-body movements. Make every movement as big and proud as possible — this is a key factor in helping your body return to normal movement patterns. What looks like an exaggeration for a healthy person is often exactly the right level of movement for someone with Parkinson’s.

  • Make every movement as large as possible — no small, cautious motions.
  • Swing your arms fully when walking.
  • Take the biggest steps you safely can.

Want a refresher on why this matters? Go back to VIGOR.

Safety first

Big doesn’t mean risky. Take the biggest steps you safely can, clear your space, and keep a sturdy chair nearby. Go at your own pace.
