Why use a workbook?

This workbook is an extra guide, designed to help you stay engaged and accountable. It isn’t required, but most participants find that having a paper copy they can write on makes the process much simpler and more concrete.

Track and measure

Use it to track and measure your results.

Stay accountable

It’s a valuable tool that encourages accountability, so you can reach the results you want.

Look back later

Refer back to it in the months or years to come. Redo the tests to see how you’ve progressed and what you may need to work on.

What’s inside

Each week of the workbook matches a module of the program. Most weeks have a to-do list to check off, and a daily log where you write in the exercise you complete each day.

WeekThemeWhat you’ll track
1Welcome!Your baseline tests (Sit to Stand, TUG, Ground Test, Single Leg Test), the PDQ-39 questionnaire, starting balance exercises daily, Goal #1 and Goal #2, and your exercise for each day.
2DiagnosisSupport Team Checklist, your current wake-up and go-to-sleep times, and a daily balance exercise.
3StressRe-write your original goals; mindfulness, journaling and gratefulness questions: What 3 things am I grateful for today? What did I fail at today, and what did I learn from failing? What am I looking forward to tomorrow?
4SleepMindfulness, journaling and gratefulness questions; your sleep schedule: wake-up time, mindfulness or journal time (10–20 minutes before bed), and go-to-sleep time.
5Steady as You Go!Foot stance video, balance exercise with Keith, ground exercise with Christine, and your daily exercise (minimum 30 minutes of high intensity).
6MotivationTry a new exercise class, a social activity with friends, and a new or old hobby, plus your daily exercise.
7Exercise #1What you need in your exercise program for Parkinson’s (V-I-G-O-R), plus daily voice, balance and 30-minute exercise.
8Exercise #22 glasses of warm water in the morning, get outside, a filled water bottle for the day, your AHA! moment for the module, plus daily voice, balance and 30-minute exercise.
9VoiceGet outside, a filled water bottle, your AHA! moment, plus daily voice, balance and 30-minute exercise, the bubbles video and facial expressions.
