This is your personal tracking tool for the whole program — and long after. Fill it in right here, week by week: tick your to-dos, write your goals, log your exercise and record your test results.

What you’ll do

  • Use one week of the workbook for each week of the program.
  • Record your baseline tests in Week 1 and re-test in Week 11 — the workbook shows your progress side by side.
  • Print it or save it as a PDF any time, filled in or blank.

Why use a workbook?

This workbook is an extra guide, designed to help you stay engaged and accountable. It isn’t required, but most participants find that writing things down makes the process much simpler and more concrete.

Track and measure

Use it to track and measure your results.

Stay accountable

It’s a valuable tool that encourages accountability, so you can reach the results you want.

Look back later

Refer back to it in the months or years to come. Redo the tests to see how you’ve progressed and what you may need to work on.
