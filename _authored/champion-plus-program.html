When a client needs more than a standard class can offer, Champion Plus is our answer. It provides one-on-one instruction, full session support, and a community that has their back — every single class. This lesson explains what Champion Plus includes, how it's priced, and what options exist when it isn't the right fit for a family.

What you'll learn

  • Explain what Champion Plus includes — dedicated one-on-one instruction, tailored exercises, real-time modifications, and full community participation.

Section 01

Our Commitment

At Boxing 4 Health, we do our absolute best to keep our clients in the program for as long as it is safe. When balance or cognitive impairment becomes a safety issue — for the client and for others in the class — we transition them into Champion Plus: a program that is safe, supported, and social for everyone.

Coach's Corner

This transition conversation is one of the hardest you'll have — but framing it as an upgrade in support, not a step down, makes all the difference. Champion Plus clients get more individual attention, more tailored exercise, and the same community they love. Lead with that.

Section 02

What Champion Plus Includes

Champion Plus is built around dedicated support for a single client throughout the entire class. Here's what that looks like in practice.

One-on-one instruction throughout the entire class

From the moment the client arrives, a dedicated instructor is assigned to them — ensuring safe entry and exit from the facility and full engagement throughout every exercise.

An hour of freedom for caregivers and spouses

Champion Plus gives caregivers the rare gift of time to themselves — errands, self-care, a coffee — while knowing their loved one is in expert, attentive hands.

Exercises tailored to their specific weaknesses

Champion Plus clients receive different exercises at different times throughout the class — targeted work on balance, freezing, coordination, and whatever their current priority area is.

Real-time modifications for injuries and limitations

Their personal instructor adapts every exercise on the fly — maximizing the value of every minute in class while keeping the client in the pain-free zone.

Full participation in the community

Champion Plus clients are not isolated or sidelined — they participate in class as much as possible. The social connection is just as important as the physical exercise, and we never let that slip.

Section 04

If Champion Plus Isn't an Option

We understand that Champion Plus may not work for every family. If that's the case, there are still options — and we will always try to help find the right path forward.

Personal Training

One-on-one personal training with a B4H instructor. Inquire directly for information and current rates. A great option for clients who need individualized attention outside of a class setting.

Online Classes (Zoom)

B4H offers online classes twice a week — Tuesdays and Thursdays via Zoom. A flexible option for clients who can't attend in person due to mobility, location, or other circumstances.

Good to know

We are here to help you make your health a top priority. We hope you can continue to join our classes. If this isn't possible, please let us know how we can help you move forward — inside or outside of Boxing 4 Health. You have options, and we are happy to help you find the right one for you and your family.

— Christine & The Boxing 4 Health Team
