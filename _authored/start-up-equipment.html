Your Path to Launch

You'll begin by securing a teaching location and diving into your online training, then move into hands-on practice with current clients while you learn. From there you'll plan your pilot program, source your equipment, and build out your referral contacts — including the neurologist outreach that's one of B4H's strongest tools for filling classes — before pulling together your marketing assets.
